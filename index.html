Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Word Quest (“the Game”) handles information when you play. The Game is provided by the Word Quest developer (“we”, “us” or “our”).

1. Playing without an account

Word Quest can be played without creating an account or signing in. We do not ask you to provide a name, email address, phone number or account password to play the Game.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The Game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements. AdMob is provided by Google. In order to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and maintain the advertising service, Google and its partners may collect or receive information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it;
  • device information, such as device model, operating system and language;
  • advertising identifiers and other device identifiers, where available;
  • information about ad impressions, ad interactions and app activity; and
  • diagnostic, performance and fraud-prevention information.

The information collected and its use depend on your device, your settings, the ads served and applicable consent requirements. Google processes information under its own privacy terms. For details, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Information collected by the developer

Word Quest does not require registration and we do not operate an account system for players. Based on the information currently provided for this policy, Google AdMob is the only third-party advertising or measurement SDK used in the Game. We do not describe AdMob-collected information as information collected directly by us; it is handled by Google under its own terms.

4. How information is used

Information processed through AdMob may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, provide rewarded-ad experiences, limit invalid activity, prevent fraud, and maintain advertising services. We do not sell players’ personal information.

5. Your choices and privacy controls

You can review or change your device’s advertising and privacy settings, including advertising identifier controls, through your Android device settings. Where consent or privacy choices are required by law, relevant choices should be made available through the Game or Google’s consent services when configured. Changing device settings may affect ad relevance or availability.

6. Children’s privacy

We do not ask players to submit personal information to create an account. Advertising services may process device and usage information as described above. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so we can review the concern. Do not send sensitive information unnecessarily.

Information processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google’s retention, security and deletion practices. We do not control Google’s systems or retention periods. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Third-party services

Google AdMob is the third-party advertising service currently identified for the Game. Its services are governed by Google’s own terms and privacy policy. We encourage you to review those documents for details about its data practices.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Game or its data practices change, or to reflect legal or operational requirements. We will update the effective date at the top when changes are made. Please review this page periodically.
